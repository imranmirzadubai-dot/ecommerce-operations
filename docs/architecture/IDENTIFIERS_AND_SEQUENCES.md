# Identifier and Sequence Contract — v4.0

## Scope

This contract defines server-generated human-readable identifiers for customers, orders, and parcels. Identifiers are database-generated, unique, and immutable. They are not gapless.

## Authoritative sequences

- `public.customer_code_seq` generates customer codes.
- `public.order_number_seq` generates order numbers.
- `public.parcel_number_seq` generates parcel numbers.

Sequences are independent. They are not shared and are not reset as part of ordinary business transactions.

## Formats

- Customer: `CUS-` + six-digit sequence value, e.g. `CUS-000001`.
- Order: `ORD-` + six-digit sequence value, e.g. `ORD-000001`.
- Parcel: `PCL-` + six-digit sequence value, e.g. `PCL-000001`.

The format is presentation/business identity, not a concurrency mechanism.

## Generation rules

1. Identifiers are generated by PostgreSQL defaults using `nextval()`.
2. The browser never supplies or calculates these identifiers.
3. `MAX(identifier)+1`, row counts, timestamps, or client-side counters must not be used.
4. Unique constraints remain authoritative against accidental collisions.
5. Generated identifiers are immutable after row creation.
6. Sequence allocation is concurrency-safe through PostgreSQL sequence semantics.

## Gaps and rollback

Sequence values are not gapless. A value may be consumed by a rolled-back transaction or otherwise remain unused. This is intentional and acceptable for the MVP. No business logic may depend on contiguous numbering.

## Retry and idempotency

A retry of a state-changing command must use command idempotency and return the original committed result rather than create a second business record. Identifier generation therefore occurs only for a genuinely new business record.

## Parcel barcode and tracking identifiers

- `parcels.barcode` is unique and constrained to equal the generated `parcel_number` in the v4.0 schema.
- `tracking_id`, when present, is globally unique through its database uniqueness constraint.
- Tracking identifiers are supplied by the applicable shipper/integration workflow, not generated from the parcel sequence.

## Invoice identifiers

Invoice numbers are independent from customer/order/parcel sequences and must remain unique. They must not reuse order or parcel sequence values as their numbering source.

## Concurrency contract

Concurrent creation of customers, orders, and parcels may consume adjacent sequence values in any order. The resulting identifiers must each be unique; ordering of sequence values is not a business guarantee.

## Prohibited behavior

- Client-side identifier generation.
- `MAX()+1` numbering.
- Count-based numbering.
- Reusing a deleted identifier.
- Updating a generated identifier to change business identity.
- Treating sequence gaps as data corruption.
- Using identifier ordering as a substitute for `created_at`, business dates, or lifecycle state.
